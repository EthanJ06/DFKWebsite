Non-Contentious Litigation:

The Court's function is not limited to resolving disputes between parties. Our team is here to guide you through the processes and make the necessary applications for obtaining the permissions and orders you require to meet your business and personal needs. These types of matters include but are not limited to:

  • Application for statutory licences;
  • Paternity orders;
  • Vesting of lands;
  • Discharge of defunct charges and restrictive covenants on land.
