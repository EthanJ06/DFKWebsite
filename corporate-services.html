Corporate Services

Managing a business brings unpredictable legal challenges. In the absence of an in-house legal team or specialty counsel, reliable guidance can be provided by DFK Corporate Services. Our General Counsel service offers flexible access to experienced business lawyers who can provide an array of legal and compliance services which focus on the administrative, structural and foundational legal needs of a business.

What We Do

  • The General Counsel service oversees legal compliance, manages risk, advises on regulatory matters, and guides corporate governance.
  • Business Life-cycle Management: Formation and Incorporation; Maturity; Restructuring; Winding down.
  • Corporate Secretarial Support: Administrative and compliance obligations to ensure good legal standing.
  • Transactional and Structural Support: The legal and administrative work needed to execute deals.

A corporate legal compliance program covers six core areas:

  1. Regulatory Filings and Reporting
  2. Data Privacy and Security
  3. Employment and Workplace Law
  4. Contracts and Commercial Compliance
  5. Anti-Corruption, Sanctions, and Trade
  6. Corporate Governance and the Board

Why Choose Us

  • All Business Types: We serve early-stage startups and established corporations.
  • Risk Management: Our GC service ensures that businesses/entities effectively balance the needs of stakeholders while maintaining operational excellence.
  • Cost-Effective: Eliminate the expensive salaries of full-time legal staff.
