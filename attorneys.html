Meet Our Attorneys

Timothy Jonas S.C.
Attorney at Law

Mr. Jonas is the Senior Attorney at the firm. He graduated from the University of the West Indies in 1994 with a Bachelor of Laws and completed his legal education at the Hugh Wooding Law School in 1996 where he attained the Legal Education Certificate.

On his return to Guyana in 1996 Mr. Jonas commenced practice as State Counsel at the Attorney General's Chambers, in the Ministry of Legal Affairs. From 1996 to 2002 he taught A-level Law at the Queen's College School, and from 1998 to 2000 was the Managing Attorney at the Bar Association's Legal Aid Clinic. In 1998 Mr. Jonas joined the firm as an Associate; where he became Partner in 2008.

During his tenure at the firm, Mr. Jonas also served as President of the Bar Association of Guyana for two consecutive terms from 2010 to 2012. From 2015 to 2018 he served as Chairman of the Guyana Competition and Fair Trading Commission. In 2015 Mr. Jonas succeeded Mr. Miles Fitzpatrick S.C. as the firm's Managing Partner. In 2020 he was appointed Senior Counsel.

"Timothy Jonas S.C. is a prominent and well-regarded litigator in Guyana's legal market. He offers in-depth understanding of constitutional and public law issues in tandem with his practice in the areas of commercial and corporate law." – Chambers and Partners Global
